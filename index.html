03WHAT TRIPS UP OPERATORS

The things that get expensive before you open.

First-time operators trip on these, and so do long-standing operators getting into the finer points of a build-out. Each one is cheaper to catch early than to fix later.

Parking ratios

Zoning can limit your seat count before a floor plan exists. It gets checked before the lease is signed.

TI allowance timing

When landlord money is paid, and what has to happen first, decides how much of the build you float yourself.

Store layout

A layout has to work on a Friday night, not just in the rendering — kitchen line, service path, bar, and storage.

Contractor management

Bids, change orders, schedules, and pay applications need someone watching them for you, every week.
